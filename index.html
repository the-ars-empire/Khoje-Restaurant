Our Heritage & Story

Welcome to Khoje Restaurant

Rooted in the Pink City's vibrant culture, bringing authentic culinary distinction to C Scheme, Ashok Nagar, Jaipur.

A Passion for Fine Gastronomy

Located in the upscale neighborhood of C Scheme, Ashok Nagar, Jaipur, Khoje Restaurant was founded with a singular vision: to create an immersive dining sanctuary where traditional Rajasthani heritage recipes harmonize seamlessly with global continental mastery.

Our master chefs handpick indigenous spices directly from local Jaipur markets, blending age-old slow-cooking techniques with modern culinary presentation. Whether you are seeking a cozy family dinner or a vibrant evening out, Khoje offers an unforgettable atmosphere with a stellar 4.7/5 rating from over 588 delighted patrons.
